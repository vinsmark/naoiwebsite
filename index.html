About Us

Your Trusted Exporter and Manufacturer in the Coconut Industry

New Asia Oil, Incorporated was first established on May, 1990. Its primary purpose is to manufacture and export Crude Coconut Oil (CNO) that is competitive in both price and quality for international market standards.

Managed by the principle and core values of I.I.H.E.A.T. (Integrity, Innovation, Humility, Excellence, and Teamwork) the company grew over time and was able to expand and venture into other coconut products such as Refined Coconut Oil (RBD), Coconut Fatty Acid Distillate (CFAD), and Cochin Oil. New Asia Oil also manufactures Copra Expeller Meal.

In addition to coconut products, the company is also one of Davao’s pioneers to invest in manufacturing Castor Oil and Castor Cake/Meal from local Castor seeds.

New Asia Oil Inc. is committed to uphold standard and quality of the products making it certified in HACCP KOSHER HALAL and Organic.

The company’s incorporators are very proud to have come so far when they have started humbly as copra traders in the 1960s. This shows that they have become very knowledgeable about the coconut industry and are committed to creating and sustaining a reputable company.

New Asia Oil, Incorporated started to export to Taiwan. Now, the company exports to different parts of the world such as Australia, China, Korea, Japan, Europe, and the US.

Fully Refined
Coconut Oil (RBD)

Read More →

A type of coconut oil that has been refined, bleached, and deodorized to remove impurities, color, and odor. It is a clear, colorless, odorless oil that is solid at room temperature. RBD coconut oil is used in a variety of food and industrial applications.

Crude
Coconut Oil

Read More →

A type of coconut oil that has been extracted from copra (dried coconut meat) but has not been refined. It is a yellowish, opaque oil with a strong coconut smell. Crude coconut oil is used in a variety of industrial applications, such as soap making and biodiesel production.

Coconut Fatty Acid Oil
Distillate Grade 1 (CFAD)

Read More →

CFAD Grade 1 is a versatile byproduct of coconut oil production. It contains valuable fatty acids and is used in various industries, including soap manufacturing, biodiesel production, and more.

Castor
Cake/Meal

Read More →

Castor Cake/Meal is a nutrient-rich byproduct derived from castor seed oil extraction. It serves as an eco-friendly soil conditioner and natural fertilizer, promoting healthy plant growth and soil vitality in agriculture.

Copra Expeller
Meal

Read More →

Copra Expeller Meal is a nutritious livestock feed ingredient produced from the residual coconut cake after oil extraction from copra. It offers high protein content, making it a valuable choice for animal nutrition and feed formulations.

Cochin
Oil

Read More →

Cochin Oil is an economical and versatile product obtained from coconut oil processing. It is used in various industries, including food production, cosmetics, and biodiesel manufacturing.

Castor Oil
Grade

Read More →

Castor Oil Grade is a high-quality castor oil product derived from castor seeds through a careful extraction process. Known for its therapeutic properties, it finds applications in industries such as pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and more.

PLANT SUPERVISOR

Basic Job Description

  1. Oversee all daily operations of the manufacturing plant, from raw materials, production, quality control, and maintenance to ensuring policies and procedures are followed.
